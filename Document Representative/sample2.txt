Artificial intelligence is transforming modern technology and everyday life.
Machine learning enables computers to learn from data without explicit programming.
Natural language processing helps machines understand and generate human language.
Text mining extracts useful information from large collections of documents.
Search engines use document indexing to provide fast and accurate results.
Data preprocessing improves the quality of information before analysis.
Stemming reduces different forms of a word to its root form.
Stop word removal eliminates common words that carry little meaning.
Information retrieval systems find relevant documents based on user queries.
Document classification organizes text into predefined categories automatically.
Recommendation systems suggest useful products, movies, or articles to users.
Big data analytics helps organizations make better decisions using large datasets.
